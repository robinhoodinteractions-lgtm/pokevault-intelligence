Research framework

Look beyond the headline price.

A useful collectible comparison considers more than a single asking price. Treat every listing as a starting point for verification, not proof of market value.

01 · Condition

Grade and preservation

Review grading company, grade, surface, centering, edges, corners, and the quality of available photos.

02 · Evidence

Comparable sales

Distinguish completed transactions from active listings, and compare like-for-like language, grade, and timing.

03 · Risk

Authenticity and liquidity

Check provenance, seller reputation, fees, shipping, returns, and how readily comparable items actually sell.

Frequently asked questions

Clear expectations.

Are the prices and market signals live?

Prices are shown only when the Scrydex provider credentials are configured and a valid raw Near Mint quote is returned. No historical observations are stored by PokéVault, and unavailable prices remain blank rather than being estimated. Research conclusions must be independently verified.

Does this guarantee a profit?

No. Collectibles can be illiquid, volatile, difficult to authenticate, and costly to buy or sell. Nothing here promises appreciation or investment returns.

Is membership or payment enabled?

No payment processor, paid membership, or customer account system is connected. Do not advertise paid access as active until it is properly configured.
